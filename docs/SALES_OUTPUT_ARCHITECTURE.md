# Sales Output Architecture

Sales output is downstream of an eligible decision, not a direct formatting
step over normalized facts. It must carry the approved scope, linked official
evidence, source snapshot identity, assumptions, limitations, and current price
context. Internal research, cross-market analysis, stale prices, and unresolved
review findings cannot be promoted to customer statements.

The Week11 Gate checks the joined customer dependency chain and the current
review authority policy. Week12 adds market isolation and integration checks.
While either Gate is `NO-GO`, downstream UI may present internal diagnostics
only; it must not generate a customer quote, sales claim, or approved objection
response. No model decision in the current pilot grants customer eligibility.
