# Customer Output Eligibility

Customer output eligibility is a separate control from scoring.

A result may become customer eligible only when all of the following are true:

- mapping was human reviewed;
- evidence package is customer eligible;
- pricing and TCO inputs are complete and fresh enough for the scenario;
- no critical conflict exists;
- scoring policy is approved for the use case;
- decision result has a real review record;
- disclaimer and approved scope are present.

Week 10 automatic decision outputs default to `internal_only`.
